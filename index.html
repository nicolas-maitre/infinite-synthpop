    How it works

    Each song is composed from a seed. Verse and chorus progressions come from a bank built on the two songs' shared Aeolian engine, i–VI–VII in the verse and i–III–VII in the chorus. The style slider moves the substitutions between them: True Faith's minor v and chorus ending on iv, against The Different Story's major V and ending on VI.

    The riff and melodic bass are per-song motifs that follow the chords. The lead melody is diatonic, mostly stepwise, with chord tones on strong beats. The final chorus doubles the lead an octave up, and some outros lift to the major key a fourth up. When a song ends, the next one starts.
