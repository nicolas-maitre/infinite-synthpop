      How it works

      Each song is composed from a seed. Verse and chorus progressions come from a bank built on True Faith's and The Different Story's shared Aeolian engine, i–VI–VII in the verse and i–III–VII in the chorus, and You Came's major IV–V loop. The style triangle blends the three: each corner is one song alone, each edge a mix of two, the centre all three. Between the first two it moves the substitutions: True Faith's minor v and chorus ending on iv, against The Different Story's major V and ending on VI; toward You Came, major keys, a pre-chorus and its drums and bass.

      The riff and melodic bass are per-song motifs that follow the chords. The lead melody is diatonic, mostly stepwise, with chord tones on strong beats. The final chorus doubles the lead an octave up, and some outros lift to the major key a fourth up. When a song ends, the next one starts.
